THE ONE CHORE THAT MATTERS
    Register for an Openverse key (or a Flickr key): a free form and one
    email click. It unlocks amateur photographs of amulets in hand and on
    stalls - exactly what a Google Lens arrival's photo looks like. If it is
    never done, nothing breaks; the 30 picture-less kinds stay at zero until
    you photograph them yourself.
